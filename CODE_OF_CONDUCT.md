# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive. Harassment, discrimination, and abusive
behavior are not tolerated.

Report unacceptable behavior to **ammari.ali.0001@gmail.com**. Maintainers will
review and act on reports in good faith and keep reporters confidential.
